0. What 010 commits you to (the filter for everything below)
========================================================================

Vision
  Students are losing work to AI: a fast, awkward generalization of
  symbolically expressed historical common knowledge.
  They will not be paid for what AI can already do cheaper.

Mission
  Transcend AI: harness, incorporate, orchestrate it.
  (Docstring also frames "defeat" as mastering what stays unexpressed.)

Strategy
  Bet on being more human: irreverent, inexpressible intuition.
  Know AI. Build YOU x AI. Surpass AI. Feed AI wisely.

Attitude
  Stay resilient. Stay human.

Baseline / Goal
  Students lack math + AI fluency, yet must deliver soon.
  Success = confidence + fundamental intuition, then self-study,
  squeeze LLMs for work, and ship.

Lecture gravity (non-negotiable design rule)
  Begin delivery, advance toward understanding -- fast.
  Habit per module: runnable demo -> name it -> just-enough math -> attempt.
  Explicitly NOT: theory first, historical zoo, delivery only at the end.

Critical success factors
  Student resilience + insightful teaching (demo before formula).

Implication for this review
  A file that is excellent as math pedagogy can still be a *curriculum*
  failure if its placement or prominence fights the gravity.
  Numbering today is archaeological. Teaching order must not follow it.

2. Missing topics (relative to an AI+DL course under YOUR mission)
========================================================================

Priority = how badly the gap fights Vision/Mission/Strategy/Gravity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
P0 -- Missing for "squeeze LLMs / deliver AI / YOU x AI"
------------------------------------------------------------------------
These are almost absent as first-class lectures, yet 010's goal
literally names them.

  1. LLM as a tool for work (not only as an architecture)
     - Prompting patterns, failure modes, verification loops
     - Using models to draft code/analysis while the student stays accountable
     - "Feed AI wisely" needs a concrete lab

  2. Retrieval / grounding / tool use (RAG, function calling, agents-lite)
     - Bridges expressed corpus knowledge vs fresh task context
     - Directly serves Mission: orchestrate AI, do not worship it

  3. A "ship a small AI" studio module
     - One thin vertical slice: data -> train or call API -> evaluate -> demo
     - Graded by attempt and explanation, not by SOTA score
     - 010's Own-it stage is stated; the corpus barely scaffolds it

  4. Fine-tuning vs prompting vs RAG decision lecture
     - When external binding (labels) is worth collecting
     - When internal binding (pretrain objectives) already suffices
     - Ties beautifully to 014 Steps 9-10

------------------------------------------------------------------------
P1 -- Missing for a honest deep-learning spine
------------------------------------------------------------------------
History (012) names these; dedicated intuition lectures do not exist.

  5. Convolution / CNN inductive bias
     - Locality, weight sharing, translation structure
     - Without this, AlexNet/ResNet in 012 are scoreboard names
     - Also preps Swin (400) properly

  6. Sequence models before Transformers (RNN / LSTM / seq2seq attention)
     - Short, demo-first bridge: why recurrence, where it hurts,
       what attention fixed
     - 260 jumps to Transformer; students miss the problem that created it

  7. Modern generative image models beyond "generative vs discriminative"
     - Diffusion (named in 012) deserves at least one intuition lecture
     - Optional: VAE as internally-bound latent binding

  8. Optimizers students will actually meet
     - Adam / AdamW (momentum 180 exists; Adam is only incidental in 080)
     - Learning-rate schedules, warmup (Transformers need this story)

  9. Overfitting / underfitting / capacity as a visual lecture
     - Scattered across 125 / dropout / inductive bias
     - Needs one memorable "see the fit fail" module

 10. Initialization + residual/highway idea outside Transformer-only 360
     - Why deep nets needed residuals (He init, ResNet story)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

     - Merge threads from 013 (levels), 220 (bias), 125 (splits),

     - Right now the idea is strong but fragmented

4. Shortcomings (structure, pedagogy, alignment -- not "bad slides")
========================================================================

S1. File order fights lecture gravity
  Low numbers are heavy on strategy + probability.
  Transformers/LLMs -- what 010 says to talk about early -- sit at 260-420.
  If anyone teaches "in numerical order," you recreate Traditional Gravity.

S2. Delivery-first is declared more than operationalized
  Many modules are beautiful intuition theaters.
  Fewer modules force a student artifact (talk track, tweak log, tiny ship).
  041_AI_fun and 016 are closest; the Transformer half rarely asks for a ship.

S3. Mission keywords under-taught as practice
  "Squeeze LLMs", "orchestrate", "unexpressible intuition",
  "expressed historical knowledge" appear in 010/012/013,
  but the middle of the course becomes standard DL encyclopedia.
  Risk: students remember losses and QKV, and still cannot run a
  YOU x AI workflow on Monday.

S4. CNN / pre-Transformer gap undercuts your own history lecture
  012 celebrates AlexNet, LSTM, etc.
  Without matching modules, history becomes museum wall, not felt constraint.

S5. Generalization is your vertical axis (013) but not a curriculum spine
  Binding (014), splits (125), bias (220), future challenges (013)
  should be one recurring chorus. Today they are separate concerts.

S6. Uneven depth
  Loss family 191-196 is lavish (good for intuition).
  Adam, CNNs, diffusion, LLM-tooling are thin or missing.
  Depth distribution still mirrors "what was fun to animate,"
  not always "what Mission needs."

S7. Companion graph is local, not global
  Files cite neighbors; there is no chapter map students can trust.
  README is essentially empty.

S8. Optional vs core is unmarked
  100/110 product/average of r.v., 116 stress test, 390/400 variants
  may be enrichment -- but nothing says so. Everything looks mandatory.

S9. 011 placement
  Valuable for Attitude / career resilience; confusing if read as DL Ch.1.
  Treat as parallel track or capstone speaking clinic.

8. Bottom line
========================================================================

You already have an unusually strong intuition library for deep learning
mechanics (loss, descent, attention, metrics) and a rare philosophical
frame (010/012/013/014/020).

The main gap is not "more math slides."
The main gap is alignment:

  (1) teaching order still looks like Traditional Gravity if numbers lead;
  (2) Mission skills (squeeze/orchestrate/deliver with LLMs) are under-built;
  (3) historical DL pillars (CNN, sequence models, diffusion, Adam) are
      thinner than the beautiful loss/attention museums;
  (4) generalization/binding is a chorus that should structure chapters,
      not only appear as late conceptual highlights.

Regroup by chapters A-P above. Keep the demos. Change the gravity of
the *sequence*, not only the slogan on slide 010.
